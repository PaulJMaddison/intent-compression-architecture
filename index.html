What It Does

ICA changes the system posture from answer-first to intent-first.

Modern LLM systems do not only need bigger context windows. They need better intent control. ICA treats ambiguity as an engineering signal: when a word, premise, or task goal carries enough uncertainty to change the answer, the system compresses that uncertainty into a small clarification or task-state update before acting.

The key rule is deliberately simple: ask only when the expected improvement in quality or safety exceeds the cost of another turn.

In retrieval-backed use cases such as UCL relationship intelligence, ICA asks before retrieval only when ambiguity changes the evidence pack or downstream action; otherwise it can retrieve under a stated assumption and keep uncertainty visible.

Architecture

A deterministic gate around probabilistic model calls.

ICA keeps orchestration explicit and auditable. The model or provider estimates ambiguity, risk, hypotheses, and candidate clarifiers; the local policy applies a thresholded routing decision before final generation.

answer_direct

Use when ambiguity is low or a clarifier would not materially change the answer.

ask_clarifier

Ask the smallest neutral question that changes the answer, evidence pack, retrieval filters, or action.

premise_check

Surface a risky, false, or impossible premise before generating a misleading answer.

refuse_redirect

Refuse or redirect when confirmation would not make literal compliance safe.

Decision Policy

The ask-vs-answer rule is a threshold, not a vibe.

Each candidate clarification gets a practical utility score. The policy asks only when the best adjusted utility clears the domain threshold tau. That keeps clarification from becoming a personality trait or a blanket delay.

Ask iff

maxq U(q | x) > τ where utility balances answer quality, safety, token cost, latency, and user friction.

Structured decision contract

  • ambiguity_score and risk_score stay separate.
  • intent_hypotheses capture competing meanings.
  • candidate_clarifiers carry expected gain and cost.
  • decision selects the route before generation.
  • answer_constraints condition the final response.
